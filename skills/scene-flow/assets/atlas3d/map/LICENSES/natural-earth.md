# Natural Earth map data licence note

The files `world.js`, `index.json`, and `relief.webp` in the parent folder are derived from Natural Earth data.
They are generated by `tools/atlas3d-map.py`, which is a developer tool and is not copied into projects.

## Licence

Natural Earth states that all versions of its raster and vector map data are in the public domain. The primary authors,
Tom Patterson and Nathaniel Vaughn Kelso, and the other contributors renounce all financial claim to the maps.
Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ (retrieved 2026-11-19).
